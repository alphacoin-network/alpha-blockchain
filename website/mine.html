How it works

  1. Get a payout address — run alpha_wallet_gen.py or getnewaddress on your node
  2. Paste it above and press Start — your browser fetches a block template and hashes it locally
  3. When a valid hash is found, it's submitted to the network — 50 ALPHA reward goes to your address
  4. Keep the tab open to keep mining. Slower on mobile, but it works — early difficulty is CPU-friendly

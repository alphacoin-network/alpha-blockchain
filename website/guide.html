Getting Started with Alpha

Alpha Coin works like Bitcoin — peer-to-peer digital money you fully control. This guide covers everything you should know before your first transaction, for individuals, businesses and developers.

Some things you need to know

If you're getting started with Alpha, there are a few things you should know. Alpha lets you exchange value in a different way than you normally do — treat it with the same care as your regular wallet, or even more.

Securing your wallet

Alpha makes it easy to transfer value anywhere while staying in control of your money — and that power comes with responsibility. Use an encrypted wallet, keep your keys private, and never share your WIF or seed. The web wallet encrypts keys with AES-256 in your browser — keys never leave your device.

There is no undo button

Alpha has no central authority to reverse a mistake. If you permanently lose access to your wallet, your funds are gone — no one, not even miners or developers, can recover them. Always back up your keys and recovery information, and keep them private.

You are your own bank

Holding your own private keys means you control your ALPHA — and you are responsible for keeping it secure. Leaving coins with a custodian or exchange introduces counterparty risk: their security and solvency stand between you and your funds. Self-custody removes that risk but requires you to secure your wallet and backups yourself.

Alpha is pseudonymous, not anonymous

All Alpha transactions are stored publicly and permanently on the blockchain — anyone can see the balance and transactions of any address. The identity behind an address stays unknown until you reveal it. Use a fresh address for each payment to protect your privacy.

Payments are irreversible

An Alpha transaction cannot be reversed — it can only be refunded by the recipient. Do business with people you trust, and use the built-in escrow contracts and multisig for larger or trust-sensitive trades. Alpha won't send to a malformed address, but always double-check the recipient.

Unconfirmed transactions aren't secure

Transactions become irreversible gradually as they accumulate confirmations — blocks are found about every 10 minutes on average. For high-value transfers, wait for more confirmations:

ConfirmationsSafety
0Only safe if you trust the payer
1–3Reliable for everyday amounts
6Recommended for high-value transfers
100Coinbase (mining) rewards maturity

Price can be volatile

The value of ALPHA can rise or fall unpredictably with adoption and market liquidity. Treat it as a high-risk asset — never store money in it that you cannot afford to lose.

Active development

Alpha is a young network evolving through active development. You may encounter changes, growing pains or bugs. Check the roadmap, consult documentation before major decisions, and remember nobody can predict the future.

Taxes and regulations

Alpha is not an official currency, but most jurisdictions still require taxes on anything of value — income, sales, or capital gains. It is your responsibility to comply with the laws of your government and local municipalities.

How to use Alpha

Getting started does not require deep technical knowledge.

1 · Inform yourself

Alpha is different from what you use every day. Read the sections above to use it securely and avoid common pitfalls.

2 · Choose your wallet

Web wallet for instant browser access (keys stay on your device), or the full-node CLI wallet (alpha_cli.py) with BIP32 HD, multi-address and AES-256 encryption.

3 · Get ALPHA

Mine it with any CPU (browser mining or mine_alpha.py), earn it for goods and services, or trade tokens for it on the built-in DEX / AMM pools.

4 · Spend ALPHA

Send to any Alpha address, scan a QR code in the web wallet, pay merchant invoices and payment links, or swap tokens — settlement in about 10 minutes.

Alpha for Individuals

Send and receive money directly, anywhere in the world.

Mobile payments made easy

Two-step scan-and-pay: display your QR code in the web wallet and let the other party scan it. No sign-up, no card, no PIN.

Security and control over your money

Transactions are secured by mathematics and energy — ECDSA and Schnorr signatures stop others spending your coins; SHA-256d proof-of-work stops anyone undoing your transactions.

Works everywhere, anytime

Like email, you don't need the recipient to use the same software — just their Alpha address. The network never sleeps, even on weekends and holidays — and nodes on the same LAN keep working even without internet.

Fast international payments

Borderless by design — no banks, no three-day waits, no minimum or maximum. The same fee whether the payment crosses the street or the planet.

Choose your own fees

No fee to receive; you pick the fee when sending. Fees depend on transaction data size, not the amount — moving a large sum costs no more than a small one.

Protect your identity

No card numbers to steal. Transactions are public but addresses aren't tied to your identity until you reveal it — use a new address per payment.

Be your own bank

Hold your keys, hold your coins. The freedom comes with responsibility — secure your wallet and backups.

More than money

Create tokens, NFTs and .alpha domains, register a did:alpha: identity, trade on the DEX, or provably burn coins — all on the same chain.

Alpha for Businesses

Accept secure payments directly from customers.

Protection against fraud

Payments are irreversible — no chargebacks, no chargeback fraud. The cost of fraud is no longer pushed onto merchants.

Merchant tools built-in

Create invoices and payment links (createinvoice), track payment status on-chain, and accept QR payments at point-of-sale — no third-party processor needed.

No PCI compliance required

You never handle sensitive customer card data. You still secure your wallet and payment requests — but skip the PCI burden.

Multi-signature

Require approval from several people to spend — e.g. a 3-of-5 board treasury (the same scheme securing the Alpha community reserve). Track who authorized what.

Accounting transparency

Every payment is verifiable on a public ledger — publish donation or revenue addresses and let anyone audit balances through the explorer.

Supply chain & certificates

Anchor product tracking events, tamper-evident certificates, audits and your business registry entry on-chain — immutable proof customers can verify.

Escrow & lending

Use on-chain escrow with arbiters and expiry, or collateralized loans, for trust-sensitive B2B deals.

Fast settlement worldwide

Cross-border payments settle in ~10 minutes for a fee you choose — ideal for remittance and international suppliers.

How does Alpha work?

The basics for a new user — a quick explanation.

Balances — the blockchain

A shared public ledger the whole network relies on. Every confirmed transaction is recorded in it, letting wallets compute spendable balances and the network verify the sender owns the coins. Its order and integrity are protected by cryptography.

Transactions — private keys

A transfer of value between wallets, signed with your private key — a secret that proves the payment came from you and can't be altered afterward. Transactions broadcast to the network and usually confirm within 10–60 minutes through mining.

Processing — mining

A distributed lottery that packs pending transactions into blocks following strict rules. It orders history, keeps the network neutral, and makes it impractical for any single party to rewrite the chain — while paying miners 50 ALPHA per block, halving every 210,000 blocks toward a 21M supply cap.

Beyond payments — the token layer

Alpha adds a meta-protocol on top of Bitcoin's design: OP_RETURN records anchor tokens, NFTs, domains, identities, oracles, supply-chain events and contracts — all validated by every full node with the same security as the coin itself. See the whitepaper for details.

Run your own node

Anyone can run a full node — permissionless, like Bitcoin. Your node downloads and independently verifies every block and transaction, then relays them to other peers. More nodes = a stronger, more decentralized network.

Requirements

Any computer works — a laptop, a Raspberry Pi, or a $5 VPS.
• Python 3.10+ (no other dependencies)
• ~1 GB RAM, ~5 GB disk (grows over time)
• Internet for sync — or LAN/offline for local operation

1 · Install & start

git clone https://github.com/alphacoin-network/alpha-blockchain
cd alpha-blockchain
python3 alphad.py --chain main --datadir ./data-mainnet

The node auto-connects to the public seeds (seed.alphacoin.network:8333, seed2.alphacoin.network:8333) and syncs the full chain — no manual peering needed.

2 · Accept inbound peers (optional)

sudo ufw allow 8333/tcp

Open P2P port 8333 so other nodes can connect to you — you become a relay that strengthens the network. The RPC port 8332 stays on localhost — never open it to the internet.

3 · Keep it running (VPS/servers)

sudo tee /etc/systemd/system/alphad.service <<'EOF'
[Unit]
Description=Alpha node
After=network.target
[Service]
ExecStart=/usr/bin/python3 /path/to/alphad.py \
  --chain main --datadir /path/to/data-mainnet
Restart=always
User=YOURUSER
[Install]
WantedBy=multi-user.target
EOF
sudo systemctl enable --now alphad

Auto-starts on boot and restarts on crash — a real 24/7 seed.

4 · Verify it's working

python3 alpha_cli.py getblockcount --datadir ./data-mainnet
python3 alpha_cli.py getconnectioncount --datadir ./data-mainnet

Height should match the explorer and connections should be > 0. To also mine: python3 mine_alpha.py --datadir ./data-mainnet --address <your address>

Why run one?

For you: trustless verification — you check the rules yourself instead of trusting a third party, plus private wallet + mining from your own node.
For the network: every node makes Alpha more censorship-resistant, harder to attack, and keeps the chain alive even if seed nodes go offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get ALPHA?

Mine it (CPU, browser or node), accept it as payment, or swap tokens on the DEX/AMM. Testnet coins are free from the built-in faucet.

How long do transactions take?

Broadcast is instant; first confirmation averages ~10 minutes. Six confirmations (~1 hour) are recommended for large amounts.

What are the fees?

You choose. Fees are based on transaction size in bytes, not value sent — typical payments cost a fraction of a cent in ALPHA.

Is ALPHA a good store of value?

It has Bitcoin's exact monetary policy — 21M cap, halving schedule, predictable issuance — but as a young asset its price can be volatile. Don't invest what you can't afford to lose.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes — a node can mine, validate, and create/sign transactions offline, and nodes on the same LAN auto-discover each other via UDP 8334. Broadcasting to the global network and chain sync need a peer connection.

Who controls Alpha?

Nobody — and everybody. It's an open-source, permissionless network like Bitcoin: miners secure it, nodes validate the rules, and no central party can change the monetary policy.

◇ Alpha Staking

Lock ALPHA on-chain, earn pro-rata rewards — enforced by Bitcoin-style CLTV script, not trust.

Honest design: Alpha is a proof-of-work chain — staking here is a lock-and-earn program, not consensus and not validator voting. When you stake, your coins are locked in a real OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY output that nobody — not even you — can spend before the unlock height. Rewards are distributed by a funder (stakedistribute) pro-rata on locked size. No new inflation: staking does not change the 21,000,000 supply cap.

How it works

  your wallet          lock tx                     maturity           claim
  ┌──────────┐   stakeopen   ┌───────────────────┐  unlock_height  ┌──────────┐
  │ 10 ALPHA │──────────────▶│ CLTV output        │────blocks──────▶│ unstake   │
  └──────────┘               │ <unlock> CLTV DROP │                 │ spends    │
                             │  DUP HASH160 <pkh> │                 │ to wallet │
                             │  EQUALVERIFY CHECK │                 └──────────┘
                             └───────────────────┘
                                   + funder runs stakedistribute → rewards
                                     paid pro-rata to all locked stakers

1 · Lock

alpha_cli stakeopen <amount> <blocks> [address]
e.g. stakeopen 100 4320 locks 100 ALPHA for ~30 days (10-minute blocks). The lock is consensus-enforced — early spend is rejected by every node.

2 · Track

alpha_cli liststakes / stakeinfo — or watch this page. Each position shows amount, unlock height and status.

3 · Earn + unlock

Rewards arrive via stakedistribute payouts pro-rata on locked size. After the unlock height: alpha_cli unstake <stake_id> returns the coins.
